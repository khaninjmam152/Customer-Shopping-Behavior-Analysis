# 🛒 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that explores customer shopping patterns and purchasing behavior using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

## 📌 Project Overview

The project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior to identify purchasing trends, revenue patterns, customer preferences, product performance, and discount usage.

The objective is to convert raw customer data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Identify customer segments based on previous purchases.
- Analyze discount usage and spending behavior.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer satisfaction through review ratings.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection
- Imported the customer shopping behavior dataset.

### 2. Data Cleaning using Python
- Explored and inspected the dataset.
- Cleaned and prepared customer data.
- Exported the cleaned dataset.

### 3. Database Management
- Connected Python with PostgreSQL.
- Stored and managed customer data.

### 4. SQL Analysis
- Revenue analysis by gender.
- Discount and spending analysis.
- Product rating analysis.
- Customer segmentation.
- Product and category performance analysis.

### 5. Power BI Dashboard
Created an interactive dashboard containing:

- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Average Purchase Amount
- Average Review Rating
- Customer Distribution by Subscription Status
- Revenue by Gender
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Age Group
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
